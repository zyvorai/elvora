# Licensing in plain words

*This page is guidance. [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers website and documentation wording in public materials. It is not legal advice.*

Elvora is **not free to use**. Published builds (control plane, agent, CLI, menu-bar app or FluxVM runner) require a commercial licence from Zyvor AI Labs.

## Price

- **$49 per enrolled Mac per year** (Cashfree checkout **₹4,899 including GST** in India; about US$49–58 depending on FX).
- One licence covers production use of **one Mac** enrolled in an Elvora fleet for twelve months.
- Licence **each Mac** you enroll. The control-plane host does not need a separate licence if it only runs `elvora-server` and is not enrolled as a worker.
- Checkout is on [Cashfree](https://payments.cashfree.com/forms/zyvor-elvora) — **India and international cards** are accepted. The invoice is your proof of licence.
- There is no licence key or in-app unlock yet. Builds are downloaded from [zyvorai/elvora](https://github.com/zyvorai/elvora/releases) or installed with Homebrew (`brew install zyvorai/tap/elvora`).

## How to buy

1. Open [Cashfree checkout](https://payments.cashfree.com/forms/zyvor-elvora) and pay for each Mac you will enroll, or
2. Write to [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev) / [zyvor.dev](https://zyvor.dev) for multi-Mac invoices, Fleet or Enterprise.

## Evaluation

Short evaluations for labs and proofs of concept are available on request via sales@zyvor.dev. Evaluation is not an open free tier.

## What a licence covers

- Running Elvora to schedule work across your enrolled Macs (Xcode CI, MLX, VMs, automation, developer machines).
- The published macOS and Linux binaries for the licensed term and Mac count.

## What it does not cover

- Redistribution of the binaries as your own product.
- Source code (the public repository does not ship source).
- macOS itself — licensed by Apple, separately.
- Support SLAs beyond what is agreed in a separate support contract.
