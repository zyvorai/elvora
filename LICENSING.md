*This page is guidance. [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the website and documentation in this repository. It is not legal advice.*

Elvora is **not free to use**. This repository publishes notarized builds, the website and documentation — not source code. Using Elvora (the control plane, agent, CLI, menu-bar app or FluxVM runner) requires a commercial licence from Zyvor AI Labs.

## Price

- **$49 per enrolled Mac per year** (about ₹4,150 exclusive of GST in India).
- One licence covers production use of **one Mac** enrolled in an Elvora fleet for twelve months.
- Licence **each Mac** you enroll. The control-plane host does not need a separate licence if it only runs `elvora-server` and is not enrolled as a worker.
- India: commercial fees are exclusive of GST; GST is added on the invoice at the applicable rate. A GST invoice is emailed and is your proof of licence.
- There is no licence key or in-app unlock yet. The notarized builds are downloaded from this repository’s Releases.

## What a licence covers

- Running Elvora to schedule work across your enrolled Macs (Xcode CI, MLX, VMs, automation, developer machines).
- The published macOS and Linux binaries for the licensed term and Mac count.

## What it does not cover

- Redistribution of the binaries as your own product.
- Source code (this repository does not ship source).
- macOS itself — licensed by Apple, separately.
- Support SLAs beyond what is agreed in a separate support contract.
